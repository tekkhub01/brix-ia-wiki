# OpenRouter

**Type:** AI model routing/aggregation platform  
**Launch:** 2024 (public data 2025–2026)  
**Model:** Aggregates multiple LLM providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Chinese models, local) behind single API  
**Pricing model:** Pay-per-token, unified billing  
**Notable 2026 trend:** Chinese models (MiniMax M2.5, Step 3.5 Flash, DeepSeek V3.2) dominated token consumption, surpassing US models for first time (February–March 2026). Chinese models priced at ~$0.30/M tokens vs Claude Opus 4.6 at $5/M.

**Token usage milestone:**
- Week of 16–22 Feb 2026: China 5.16T vs USA 2.7T
- Week of 24 Feb 2026: Chinese models = 61% of top-10 token share (8.7T total)
- Week of 9–15 Mar 2026: China 4.69T vs USA 3.29T

**Top 3 global models (100% Chinese):**
1. MiniMax M2.5
2. Step 3.5 Flash
3. DeepSeek V3.2

**Strategic implication for BRIX-IA:** Price pressure drives local deployment. With 80% of US AI startups using open-source Chinese models as base, on-premise agents built on GLM 5.1 + RotorQuant become economically inevitable.
